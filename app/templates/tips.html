Food Saving Tips

Simple ways to use food better.

Simple habits can help you use food better, save money and reduce unnecessary food waste.

Before You Shop
  • 1. Check My Food
  • 2. See what needs to be used soon
  • 3. Plan a few meals
  • 4. Buy only what you need
Check My Food Plan a Meal
Store Food Better
  • •Keep perishable food in the refrigerator when needed.
  • •Keep older food in front so it gets used first.
  • •Store food in clean and closed containers.
  • •Check expiry and best-before dates regularly.
  • •Keep fruits and vegetables properly stored.
Use Leftovers Better
  • •Check usable leftovers before cooking a new meal.
  • •Plan the next meal around food already available.
  • •Store usable leftovers safely.
  • •Use suitable leftovers in another meal when safe.
  • •Avoid cooking more than the household usually needs.
Safety Note: Do not eat or reuse food that looks or smells unsafe.
Simple Leftover Ideas
  • Cooked rice:
    Can be used in another suitable rice dish if it has been stored safely.
  • Vegetables:
    Can be included in the next suitable meal.
  • Bread:
    Can be used for toast or another simple dish if still safe to eat.
  • Fruit:
    Use ripe fruit soon or include it in a suitable snack/drink.
Safety Note: Food should only be reused when properly stored and still safe.

Dispose Food Responsibly

  • • Separate wet food waste from dry waste where possible.
  • • Compost suitable food waste if composting is available.
  • • Follow local waste collection rules.
  • • Give suitable food to animals only when it is safe and appropriate.
  • • Do not donate spoiled or unsafe food.
Note: Food that is spoiled or unsafe should not be eaten or donated.
